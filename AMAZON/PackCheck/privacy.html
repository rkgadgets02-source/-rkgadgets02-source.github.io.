Privacy Policy

Effective 3 November 2026

This policy covers the PackCheck app, published by RK Bazaar.

In short: PackCheck keeps everything on your phone. We do not collect, upload, sell or share any of your data. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

Camera

PackCheck uses your camera only to scan shipping labels and product barcodes, and to photograph the Order ID on a label when you ask it to. Text is read from the photo on your phone. Camera images are never saved by the app or sent anywhere.

Your orders and sales data

When you import an orders file from Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho or Shopify, the app reads it on your phone. It keeps only what it needs for packing and sales: order IDs, dates, order status, SKUs, product names, quantities, prices and tracking/AWB numbers. It does not keep buyer names, addresses, phone numbers or emails.

This data, along with your fee settings and saved barcodes, is stored only in the app's storage on your device. It never leaves your phone unless you choose to export a CSV file or download a backup, and then it goes only where you put it.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect any personal or usage data.
  • We do not upload or share your data with anyone.
  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising.
  • No analytics, tracking or cookies.

Downloading the app

The app's own files are downloaded from the website that hosts it, like any web page. That host may keep standard technical logs (such as IP addresses) for security. We do not receive or use these logs. The app loads nothing from any other website.

Deleting your data

Open More → Delete all data in the app, or uninstall the app. Because we hold no copy, we cannot recover deleted data, so keep backups if you need them.

Children

PackCheck is a business tool for sellers and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
